Midterm szn needs a break

McCup is a one-day student event on the Lower Field. Teams take on silly challenges, everyone else watches, eats and hangs out.

Anyone can play

The challenges are silly, not skilled. Grab 3 to 5 friends, pick a team name and show up.

Great to watch

Not competing? Bring your friends and cheer. Watching your friend eat a lemon with a straight face is half the fun.

Stay and chill

Drinks and food in the chill zone give you a reason to stay all day, and the money goes to charity.

Pick your challenges

Some are mandatory, most are optional. Do as many as you want. It stays chill, not extreme.

How McCup works

Every challenge earns points, and the leaderboard updates live on a big screen.

  • Teams of 4 to 6 sign up ahead of time with a team name.
  • Each challenge is worth points. Pick the ones you want to try.
  • Spirit points go to the best costumes, chants and biggest cheering section.
  • A DJ or student band keeps the energy up between rounds.
  • One team wins the McCup at the end of the day.
